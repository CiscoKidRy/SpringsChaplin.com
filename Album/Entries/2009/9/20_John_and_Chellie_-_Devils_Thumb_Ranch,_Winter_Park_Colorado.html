John and Chellie - Devil’s Thumb Ranch, Winter Park Colorado

 

My husband and I have were married in Sept 2009 and "Pastor Paul" (as we affectionately call him) officiated our ceremony. He was so easy to work with!!! We shared with Paul how important it was for us to write our own ceremony and vows and he provided us with all sorts of different resources and guidance in this process. He worked with us to create a very meaningful ceremony that incorporated both traditional and modern elements, exactly what we wanted. Paul attended our rehearsal, helped keep things organized during the rehearsal, and spent the time to make sure that everyone felt comfortable with the process so that when it came time for the actual ceremony we could relax and enjoy ourselves (and not have to worry about what was coming next & who needed to be where). Paul is such a compassionate and authentic person, it is evident that he really cares about the people that he works with and that is exactly what we wanted in an officiant. We were absolutely thrilled to have Paul officiate our wedding ceremony and recommend him without any reservation!

 

September 2009
