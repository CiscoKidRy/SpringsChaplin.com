Jamie and Nick - Cielo at Castle Pines

 

I can honestly say that because of Chaplain Paul Anderson, and a few kind hearted and wonderful people like him, May 18, 2012 was a dream come true! My husband Nick and I were first married at a courthouse on February 26, 2004 just 4 days before he deployed to Iraq. We made plans to have a wedding when he returned. That changed when he was severely injured 2 months later and was medically retired from the U.S. Army. Our priorities changed and we put our wedding on the back burner. In 2011 some friends decided to change that, they called Cielo at Castle Pines and asked if they would be willing to help put together a wedding for us. They immediately said yes and the planning began. I was told in January to pick a date. The first time I met with Roz at Cielo she told me if we wanted someone to officiate she knew the perfect guy for the job. At first we weren’t sure since we were already legally married, we didn’t need anyone official. We decided to call and meet him before we made a decision. The first time we talked to him we knew we had to have him officiate our ceremony. He was the perfect guy for our wedding! He helped us write a ceremony that was just right for us. As crazy (wonderful) as that day was for me one of the nicest memories was when Chaplain Paul walked up to me with a hanger full of ties and asked which one I wanted him to wear. It showed how much he truly cared about making this day perfect for me. Thank you Paul for making our wedding so truly beautiful and for letting us meet your beautiful wife. Nick and I are blessed to have such wonderful people in our lives.
