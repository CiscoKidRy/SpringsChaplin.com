These are the qualities I want for you as you begin your life together as husband and wife. If chosen to officiate at your wedding, I will not only celebrate with you on that day, but will be available to assist you in planning an experience that is personal.  We will make sure that it expresses your thoughts and feelings about each other. I will work with you so that what is said and done during the ceremony through your vows, prayers, readings, music, etc. is what you wish to share with each other, your family and friends. I will assist you as you bring together your respective families, who may or may not have met previously, to create an atmosphere where all feel welcome and comfortable. I will be at any rehearsal to insure that all participants feel comfortable in their roles and can relax and enjoy the entire day.

I have been married for thirty-eight years. My wife, Patricia, and I have three sons, three daughters-in-law, and five wonderful grandchildren. I am very comfortable with non-traditional and non-denominational weddings. The majority of my ministry has been as a Protestant chaplain, serving in the Air Force having retired in 1999.  I have performed weddings at the Air Force Academy Cadet Chapel, The Broadmoor Hotel, Pine Crest, and a variety of other resorts and locations throughout the United States.

Should you decide you would like me to officiate, I would truly consider it an honor to be a part of your special day!  May God richly bless you as you take this journey together!
